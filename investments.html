The Real Estate Fund Investments

Multi-Family Real Estate Disciplined Focus
Based on the Founder’s experience the focus will be to identify properties that are miss-managed and in need of limited or extensive renovation. The Real Estate Fund's goal is to provide attractive housing for tenants seeking a safe environment they can be proud of.
We focus on multi-family properties in the 24-60 unit range, which represent a significant piece of the market where we expect to find opportunities. It is also a segment of the market where once we decide to sell, we should receive offers at the higher end of an improving market. As we grow, larger properties will be considered in our Business Plan.
